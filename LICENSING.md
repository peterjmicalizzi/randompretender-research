# Initial publication and reuse

The owner has not yet selected a broad reuse license for this library. Public visibility
does not automatically grant an MIT or Creative Commons license. Viewing and forking on
GitHub are subject to GitHub's terms; do not assume permission for other redistribution
or commercial reuse of contributed material.

Contributors retain their rights. Only submit work you are authorized to publish publicly
and have included in this repository. Identify any existing license and third-party source.
Do not copy arXiv PDFs, private application code, or other people's material without the
necessary permission.

A possible future policy is CC BY 4.0 for original narrative/data and MIT for original code.
That remains a decision for the owner and contributors. It would apply only to material
whose rights holders authorize it, never automatically to earlier contributions or the
private application. Until that decision, this repository should be described as a public
research workspace, not as fully open-source licensed software.
